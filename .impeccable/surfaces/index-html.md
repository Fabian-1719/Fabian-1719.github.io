# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: the single-page personal portfolio at https://fabian-1719.github.io. Visitor mode: Experience. Replacement world (2026-11-18): the first build's vector Night to Day landscape was rejected as tied to the GitHub banner and too polygonal.
Audience: professors, classmates and developers arriving from GitHub, LinkedIn or a shared link. Job: see what Fabian builds, open code or demos, reach him. Proof: the real projects in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, EN/ES switch, GitHub Pages, reduced motion respected, background art detailed and made in code.
Memorable moment: a hand-built pixel world whose day runs out as you scroll, water and fire alive through palette cycling, ending at a campfire under the stars.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A pixel world that lives a full day while you read. The camera walks one long original landscape from a cliff-top tower at morning to a hill campfire at night; it refuses the dark hero plus card grid and the vector landscape it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: An indexed-colour canvas at low resolution (about 180 to 200 logical pixels tall, integer-scaled, nearest-neighbour). Every pixel belongs to a palette role (sky bands, far range, hills, four leaf tones with outline, rock, soil, grass, water and waterfall cycle groups, fire, stone, roof, window glow); time of day remaps the whole palette through day, golden, dusk and night keys, and cycle groups rotate to animate water, falls and fire. Ordered (Bayer) dithering instead of gradients. Content sits in framed pixel panels in Pixelify Sans headings and Atkinson Hyperlegible body; gold is reserved for what can be clicked. Zelda nods stay small: sword in a pedestal in a forest clearing, a three-triangle constellation at night, a fairy that follows the pointer.

STORY: Morning at the tower: who he is. The lake and waterfall: about. The forest walk past the sword: the projects. The village at sunset: his stack. Night at the campfire: contact with email, LinkedIn and GitHub one click away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed pixel scene at morning: warm morning palette key, cliff plateau with the stone tower right of centre, distant peaks and cumulus clouds, the sun low to the right of the tower. Left column holds a framed panel: name very large in Pixelify Sans, one-line positioning, a gold "See the projects" action and the GitHub link. Top bar with handle, section links and EN/ES. A small pixel clock at 07:00, bottom-right on wide screens (it never covers the left reading column) and centred in the top bar on phones.

FORM: "Pixel art with palette cycling", position 1 of my ordered list (taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK). seed: f1ad000302200fdc. Signature interaction: scroll drives both the camera pan across the world and the time of day (07:00 to 22:00), with live palette cycling for water, falls and fire. Motion grammar: palette cycling and sprite loops at a deliberate low frame rate; scroll-linked camera; no entrance animations on panels; a spring-smoothed fairy on hover devices; reduced motion freezes cycling and sprites but keeps scroll-driven time.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
