# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: the single-page personal portfolio at https://fabian-1719.github.io. Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience: professors, classmates and developers arriving from GitHub, LinkedIn or a shared link. Job: see what Fabian builds, open code or demos, reach him. Proof: the real projects in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, EN/ES switch, GitHub Pages, reduced motion respected.
Memorable moment: the sun rising over Arenal as you scroll, the clock ticking from 00:00 to 12:00.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Scroll is time. The page opens at midnight over Arenal and ends at noon; it refuses the dark hero plus a grid of project cards that every developer portfolio ships.

OWN-WORLD: Layered landscape silhouettes (basalt ridges, Arenal cone with smoke, palms) are the page's grounds; a fixed sky crossfades night navy to dawn ember to day blue; the projects live on a solid jungle-green hillside, not on cards. Lava orange marks only what is clickable. Display and body in Bricolage Grotesque, labels and the clock in JetBrains Mono. Fireflies at night become butterflies by day; subtle Zelda nods (three-triangle constellation, sword in a pedestal, a small blue fairy).

STORY: The visitor learns who Fabian is in the night sky, walks a trail of real projects through dawn, sees his stack in the morning, and reaches a noon contact with email, LinkedIn and GitHub one click away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night landscape. Name set very large in the upper sky, one-line positioning under it, an orange "See projects" action and a GitHub link below. Moon upper left, constellation upper right, fireflies over the ridge. Minimal top bar: handle left, section links and ES/EN right. A small mono clock fixed bottom-left reads 00:00.

FORM: "Night to Day", position 1 of my ordered list (taken as IMPECCABLE'S PICK). seed: 10aea50371bb0dd3. Signature interaction: the scroll-driven sky crossfade with the moon and sun travelling one arc and the live time-of-day clock. Motion grammar: ambient CSS loops for stars, fireflies and smoke; scroll-linked opacity and transform only; content enters once with short ease-out reveals; a spring-smoothed fairy follows the pointer on hover-capable devices.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
