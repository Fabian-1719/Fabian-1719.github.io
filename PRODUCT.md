## Stack

## Users

Professors, classmates and the wider developer community who want to see what Fabian builds. Not an active job search (no recruiter funnel, no freelance offer). Visitors arrive mostly from his GitHub profile, LinkedIn, or a link shared in conversation.

## Product Purpose

A personal showcase for Fabian Aragón Arce, Computer Engineering student at Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica (TEC). It presents who he is, the projects he has built and what he is learning. Success: a visitor leaves knowing the projects, remembering the site, and with an easy way to reach him or open the code.

## Product Principles

1. Show real work, described truthfully and concretely.
